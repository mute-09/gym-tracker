from the **Everkinetic** open data project.

## Modifications

The original `dist/svg` files provide two poses per exercise (`relaxation` and
`tension`). For each exercise used by this app, those two SVG frames were:

- stripped of their white background layer;
- rasterized at 360×360 on a white canvas;
- combined into an animated GIF (`ek-<id>.gif`, 1 second per frame); and
- flattened to a JPG still (`ek-<id>.jpg`).

The resulting GIF/JPG files are adaptations and are distributed under the same
**CC BY-SA 4.0** license. Attribution: **Everkinetic — https://everkinetic.com**.

## Not included

This repository no longer contains any media from Gym visual / gymvisual.com.
Exercises without an available Everkinetic illustration (for example cardio
machine movements) intentionally have no media file.
